SenseTime says that after Design Arena’s image boards updated, **SenseNova U1 Pro** ranked **#1 in China** on both Image generation and Image Editing, and **#6 globally**, with GPT Image 2.x variants occupying the top five. The post also cites a preference-vs-price view where U1 Pro pairs strong user preference with competitive generation cost.

Design Arena is described as same-prompt generation, anonymous pairwise human votes, and Elo-style dynamic ranking. SenseTime further stresses long-horizon creative pipelines beyond single-image quality. Treat every placement as a company recount of a third-party board—**name the board, update time, and submission status** when citing; this article does not convert “#1” into an unauditable absolute.
