OpenAI’s **Business Pricing** page is the enterprise/commercial entry point for ChatGPT Work / Codex and API-credit navigation. This piece maps the public structure—**not** a hike notice. Dollar seat prices belong on the live plan cards.

## How to read the two tiers

| Plan | Public framing |
| --- | --- |
| **Business** | Secure workspace; mix-and-match seats; Google Workspace / Slack / GitHub / M365 connectors; SAML / SSO / MFA; central billing and spend controls; no training on business data by default; workspace agents; copy cites up to ~**$500**/month API credits for the workspace |
| **Enterprise** | Custom sales pricing; expanded context; SCIM, EKM, RBAC, data residency (ten regions cited), SLAs / custom legal terms |

FAQ: personal Free / Go / Plus sit elsewhere; education and nonprofit discount paths are separate. Copy says starting in **December**, plan usage will share a limit across ChatGPT and Codex—check the linked “Learn more” before deploy.

## Modeling tip

Split **seat fees** from **API / credit burn**. Business and Enterprise can buy more credits for access. Model names (GPT-6.x Sol / Astra, etc.) and context-window figures move with product updates—cite the live comparison table, not a cached screenshot, in contracts.
