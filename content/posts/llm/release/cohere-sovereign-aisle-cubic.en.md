Cohere (**13 Nov 2026**) marks two sovereign-AI milestones: **closing the AISLE acquisition** (autonomous vulnerability discovery and remediation) and **welcoming the cubic** AI code-review team. After Aleph Alpha and Reliant AI, the company says it has completed **four** strategic M&A deals this year spanning frontier AI, biopharma, cybersecurity, and software development.

AISLE: co-founders take SVP Cyber (Ondrej Vlcek), CISO (Jaya Baloo), and VP AI Security (Stanislav Fort) roles; AISLE’s stack seeds **Cohere Cyber**, with a planned long-term Prague expansion. cubic: founder-CEO Paul Sanglé-Ferrière leads a secure-coding vertical built for complex codebases, vuln review, and on-prem/air-gapped deployments. M&A close note—no ship dates or pricing for the combined products.
