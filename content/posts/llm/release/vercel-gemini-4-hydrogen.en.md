Vercel’s Changelog adds Google **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** to **AI Gateway** as `google/gemini-4-hydrogen`. It positions Hydrogen as the successor to **Gemini 3.8 Flash** in a similar latency class, with stronger agentic coding, knowledge work, and tool use—plus tighter adherence to system instructions, formats, and task boundaries, and fewer unsupported claims in search-backed or coding reports.

Call it from the AI SDK or wire coding agents with `npx vercel ai-gateway setup`, then try the playground. Capability and pricing detail live in Google’s launch post; this is a **Gateway availability** note.
