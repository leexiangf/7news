OpenAI’s release (**18 Nov 2026**) announces a strategic partnership with **Le Groupe Figaro** so journalism from Le Figaro and associated titles—news, business, culture, and lifestyle, current and archive—becomes discoverable in ChatGPT through **summaries and excerpts**, including links to the underlying stories.

Figaro **retains full editorial independence and control**. The publisher also plans to roll out **ChatGPT Enterprise** across teams and explore further workflow and reader-facing uses under its AI charter and editorial standards. Publisher partnership note—no revenue share or API plumbing disclosed.
