OpenAI (**16 Nov 2026**) expands **ChatGPT for Academic Researchers**. Applications for the next round open **23 Nov** with **50,000** first-come grants for eligible researchers (no lottery). Each grant covers the recipient plus up to two collaborators on **ChatGPT Plus**—up to about **150,000** people this round—toward a program goal of **200,000**. Data shared through the program is not used for training by default.

The July pilot reached about **20,000** researchers in **33** countries; usage mostly fit Plus limits, so new grants move from Pro to Plus to stretch coverage. A companion *Science at the Frontier* study and pointers to ChatGPT Edu / GPT-Rosalind sit alongside. Grant-program update.
