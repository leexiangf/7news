Moonshot open-sources **Kimi K2.6**, available on **Kimi.com, the App, API, and Kimi Code**. The pitch centers on **long-horizon coding**, **Agent Swarm**, and reliability for always-on agents such as OpenClaw and Hermes. Versus K2.5, public tables cite SWE-Bench Pro about **58.6 vs 50.7** and Terminal-Bench 2.0 about **66.7 vs 50.8** (see footnotes for settings).

Showcases include deploying and tuning Qwen3.5-0.8B inference in Zig on a Mac across **4,000+** tool calls and ~**12 hours** (~**15→193 tok/s**), and a ~**13-hour** overhaul of the exchange-core matching engine with roughly **+185%** medium throughput. Swarm scale rises from K2.5’s ~100 agents / 1,500 steps to about **300** sub-agents and **4,000** coordinated steps, with high-quality files turned into Skills; **Claw Groups** appears as a research preview for heterogeneous collaboration. The page also lists **Kimi K3** as stronger on enterprise benches—pick open K2.6 vs higher-tier K3 deliberately. Figures are vendor or partner-reported.
