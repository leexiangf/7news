SenseTime’s English news (**20 Nov 2026**) says that during the **18–19 Nov** 33rd APEC Economic Leaders’ Informal Meeting in Shenzhen, the company showed AI products at exchange events and urban venues under themes of openness, innovation, and cooperation. Treat it as an **event showcase**, not a specs sheet or model launch.

## Venues and exhibits (named in the release)

| Venue | Exhibit / experience |
| --- | --- |
| Foreign Guests’ Lounge, Shenzhen International Exchange Center | **SenseRobot** desktop chess robot (vision + arm)—one of 10+ selected tech exhibits |
| Qianhai Waves and urban reception spaces | **Raccoon Work** for spreadsheet work; **Seko** text-to-video creation flow |
| On-site interaction | ACE Robotics “Xiaotu” quadruped patrol/guidance (nav / obstacle avoidance) |
| City spots | Ping An Finance Center, Talent Park, Longgang AI 6S Store also hosted SenseRobot |
| Forum | Invited to the APEC University Presidents’ Forum |

Closing copy recalls the 2014 Hong Kong founding and 2015 Qianhai presence—Greater Bay Area narrative. Whether any exhibit ships a new model version or a commercial SKU is not independently listed—do not upgrade booth demos into product GA.
