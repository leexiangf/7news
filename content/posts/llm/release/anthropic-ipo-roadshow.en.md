Anthropic, PBC launched the roadshow for its proposed **initial public offering**. The deal contemplates **825,000,000** shares of Class A common stock—about **801.35** million from Anthropic and **23.65** million from selling stockholders (Anthropic receives no proceeds from those sales). Underwriters are expected to get a 30-day option for up to about **123.75** million additional shares.

The IPO price is expected between **$70.00 and $78.00** per share. Anthropic has applied to list Class A shares on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under **ANT**. The company stresses the registration statement is **not yet effective**; today’s note is not an offer to sell, and any proposed offering would proceed only by prospectus.
