OpenAI reports that more than **20,000** transitioning servicemembers and veterans have claimed a **free year of ChatGPT Plus** in the past year. The post highlights job-search prep, translating military experience into civilian résumés, interview practice (including voice mode), and publishing workflows, plus more than a dozen OpenAI Academy partner trainings for nearly 1,000 people in the military community—with more sessions scheduled.

Treat it as a **community program update**, not a model release; eligibility and claim links live in the original post. Dated **2026-11-11** per the source page.
