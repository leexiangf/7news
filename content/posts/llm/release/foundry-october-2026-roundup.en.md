Microsoft Foundry published its **October 2026** what’s-new digest—a map of agent-boundary updates (follow linked posts for detail).

**Agents and tools.** The **Microsoft Copilot harness** enters public preview: a managed loop with skills and supported tools. **Long-running hosted-agent resilience** is GA (recovery still needs app checkpoints). **Browser Automation** is GA via Playwright Workspaces. **Decisions API** and **GPT-6.1 Terra** land in the GA narrative; MAI Image 2.6 preview and streaming/multilingual audio items appear as well.

**Local and tooling.** Foundry Local notes SDK/runtime **2.2.1** and a separate CLI preview—verify changed CUDA dependencies before upgrading GPU apps. The digest warns that October SDK/CLI updates rename preview APIs and evaluation commands; upgrade deliberately. An Ignite (Nov 17–20) Foundry session table is included for planning.
