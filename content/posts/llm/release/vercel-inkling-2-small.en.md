Vercel’s Changelog adds Thinking Machines’ **Inkling 2 Small** to **AI Gateway** as `thinkingmachines/inkling-2-small`. It frames a multimodal reasoning model—text and image in, text out—for coding agents, visual understanding (charts, UIs), and turning tool-gathered information into documents and spreadsheets; for visual development it can inspect screenshots of its own UI output and revise.

Call it from the AI SDK or wire agents with `npx vercel ai-gateway setup`. Architecture and self-host numbers live in vLLM’s same-day day-0 post; this note is **Gateway availability** only.
