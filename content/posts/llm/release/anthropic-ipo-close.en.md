Anthropic (**20 Nov 2026**) announced the **closing** of its initial public offering, including the underwriters’ **full exercise** of the overallotment option. This piece marks closing and listing; roadshow and pricing detail live in earlier posts.

## IPO structure (company release)

| Item | Figure |
| --- | --- |
| Shares offered | ~**1.127B** Class A (including ~**147M** overallotment) |
| Public offering price | **$83.00** per share |
| Sold by Anthropic | ~**1.10335B** shares |
| Sold by stockholders | ~**23.65M** shares (no proceeds to the company) |
| Aggregate gross (pre-fees) | ~**$93.5B** |
| Proceeds to Anthropic (pre-fees) | ~**$91.6B** |

Class A began trading on Nasdaq Global Select on **19 Nov 2026** under **ANT**. The registration statement was declared effective on **18 Nov**.

## Concurrent private placement

~**313.25M** shares of **Class N** went to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, NVIDIA, and TSMC Partners at the same **$83.00**, for ~**$26.0B**—unregistered under the Securities Act. Combined IPO (including stockholder sales) and private placement gross ~**$119.5B** before fees.

Lead bookrunners: Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley; additional bookrunners and co-managers are listed in the release. All figures are company-stated, not third-party valuations.
