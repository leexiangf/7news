## Bottom line first

Hy4 is good enough for short delivery cycles: a runnable prototype in about an hour, plus a planning outline that holds together. What stalls is default product judgment—layout, tone, and error copy that sound like *your* product, not a generic assistant.

## How we tested

Closed task: build a minimum UI for an internal tool and one page of feature notes. Inputs were target users, must-have features, and hard constraints only—no existing design.

Watch points:

1. Does it clarify missing constraints on its own?
2. Does it emit runnable code, not a shell?
3. Is the copy fit to show users as-is?

## Result sketch

- **Engineering:** Path runs; component boundaries are clear.
- **Planning:** Structure is complete; CTAs and empty states lean template.
- **Product feel:** Needs a human second pass for cuts and brand fit.

## Suggested use

Park Hy4 in draft-and-accelerate. Keep publish gates with people and eval sets. If your bottleneck is hands, not ideas, it helps. If you lack product standards, the model alone will not invent them.
