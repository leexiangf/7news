ByteDance Seed’s blog (byline **16 Nov 2026**; site entry **20 Nov**) opens the **Seed STEM Plan | Molecular Retrosynthesis Challenge**. The theme is chemical retrosynthesis: plan efficient, robust routes to a target molecule—what decides whether a candidate is usable in pharma or materials. The earlier Seed STEM scientist program already partnered with ~**100** scholars on AI-accelerated discovery.

## Task and prizes

| Item | Claim |
| --- | --- |
| Problems | **10** real, hard retrosynthesis items; structure given; design a full route on **Volcano Engine (火山方舟)** |
| 1st prize | 1 winner, **¥50,000** |
| 2nd / 3rd / merit | 2×¥30k, 3×¥10k, 10×¥5k |
| Extras | Official Seed STEM certificate; Doubao Work personal premium month card |

## Timeline and bounds

- **Eligibility**: Mainland China only; any degree; targets organic-synthesis experience plus day-to-day AI tooling  
- **Register**: now through **4 Dec 12:00**  
- **Problems / submit**: published **7 Dec**; due **10 Dec**  
- **Judging**: expert pre-screen → independent CRO (**WuXi AppTec / 睿智医药** named) **wet-lab validation** → combined review  

Questions: `seedSTEM@bytedance.com`; registration form linked in the post. For science-tooling readers: a public Seed science-AI contest signal—eligibility and scoring live on the official pages.
