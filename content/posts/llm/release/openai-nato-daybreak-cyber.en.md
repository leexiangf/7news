OpenAI (**17 Nov 2026**) outlines putting advanced models in trusted defenders’ hands through **Daybreak** for authorized security work—reviewing software, investigating suspicious activity, validating vulnerabilities, and developing/testing fixes. With NATO’s **NCIA**, it supplies cyber models to core Alliance institutions and cites usage of up to about **300 billion** raw input and output tokens per month.

It also supported the NATO CCDCOE **2026 Crossed Swords** exercise’s new stream where participants built, deployed, and evaluated AI agents for cyber ops with human oversight. Allied callouts include the EU’s **ENISA** (four vulnerabilities found and fixed in EU-institution software, plus a joint ENISA/CERT-EU review), Ukraine’s Ministry of Digital Transformation on civilian infrastructure, and **CERT Polska**’s MikroTik RouterOS disclosures. This is a **policy/partnership** note, not a general API feature ship; scope stays inside Daybreak authorizations.
