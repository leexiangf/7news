SenseTime announced the official **SenseNova 6.8 Flash** multimodal agent model for office intelligence and complex real-world tasks. The pitch centers on **long-term memory, proactive interaction, and cross-task continuity**, with demos such as resuming a paused customer opportunity and handing work context to a new teammate.

On the design side, generation moves beyond static assets into dynamic PPT layouts, animated posters, Blender 3D scenes, Web/SVG, and Three.js pages, with optional Figma-oriented workflows. 3D paths need a local environment and multi-round interaction. SenseTime says the model is strong on agent collaboration, data analysis, and presentation generation and “outperformed some leading international models” on multiple benchmarks—vendor framing without itemized scores here.

Free trial access runs through the SenseNova Token Plan: up to **1,500** free API requests every five hours via [platform.sensenova.ai](https://platform.sensenova.ai/).
