SenseTime issued a business update for the nine months ended 30 September 2026 (EN and CN posts aligned). Financials: revenue **+30.4%** YoY to **RMB 3.79B**; gross margin up **4.3** ppt to **43.4%**; gross profit **+45.0%** to **RMB 1.65B**. Generative AI is framed as the growth core, with Q3 YoY growth said to **more than double**.

Product notes mention SenseNova **6.8 Flash**, **U1 Pro**, **Loop 9B**, **Code2Video**, and **RoboRSI**. Infra claims include a 10k-GPU-class heterogeneous inference cluster, roughly **5×** token throughput, and SenseCore daily tokens from over **2.4T** in July to **6T** in October, plus certifications and Neocloud share language. Agent and vision commercial metrics are also company-stated.

This merge of the official EN/CN releases only restates listed figures and names. Rankings and “#1” claims are corporate announcements; verify elsewhere before treating them as independent fact. Investors should rely on HKEX disclosures.
