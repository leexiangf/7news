Anthropic, PBC said on 18 November 2026 that it priced its initial public offering of Class A common stock at **$83.00** per share. This is a capital-markets announcement, not a model or API release; the notes below stick to terms stated on the company news page.

The offering covers **980,000,000** Class A shares: Anthropic is selling **956,351,354**, and selling stockholders are selling **23,648,646**. Anthropic states it will **not** receive proceeds from the stockholder sales. Underwriters also received a **30-day** option to buy up to **147,000,000** additional shares.

Trading is expected to begin on **19 November 2026** on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol **“ANT.”** Closing is expected on **20 November 2026**, subject to customary conditions. The post says a registration statement was declared effective by the SEC the same day, that the offering is made only by prospectus, and lists Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley contacts for the final prospectus when available. It also includes the standard disclaimer that the announcement is not an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy in any jurisdiction where that would be unlawful before registration or qualification.
