OpenAI (**17 Nov 2026**) launches **GPT-6.2 Astra** and **GPT-6.2 Sol**. Astra targets the hardest multi-step professional work—slides, spreadsheets, and docs that follow your templates and field norms, plus cleaner code and stronger frontends for sites, games, and 3D. Sol is the everyday coding, writing, and agent default, framed as smarter and more token-efficient than **GPT-6.1 Sol**.

Vendor-reported highlights: on Agents’ Last Exam, Astra is about **5.2** points above **GPT-6 Astra** at under a third of the cost; Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 is roughly **9** points higher. API list prices are **$8 / $0.40 / $40** per million in / cached in / out for Astra, and **$2 / $0.10 / $10** for Sol. Safety notes say both hit Preparedness **Cyber Critical** and bio/chem **High**, with Astra the first external release at **AI Self-improvement High**; tighter cyber refusals accompany stronger defensive training, while **Daybreak** still centers prior models pending 6.2 access. Shipping in ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API with **Ultrafast** for both. Treat benches and cost ratios as OpenAI/partner reports and keep harness footnotes when quoting.
