OpenAI (**17 Nov 2026**) updates **Astra for Law**—a **GPT-6.2 Astra** configuration with an expanded legal search index and instructions for research, weighing authorities, and drafting. Vendor figures: about **20.8%** full-credit on Harvey **LAB-AA** versus **15.0%** for plain GPT-6.2 Astra (~**40%** relative), ~**0.03** material hallucinations per assignment; Vals AI Legal Research Bench full-credit ~**46%→53.8%** (16 Nov eval); index up ~**34%** since September to **310M+** URLs.

After a September pilot with select firms, every **ChatGPT Enterprise** customer can request access via the **Trusted Access Program**; enabled workspaces pick `gpt-6.2-astra-law`. API access is a separate request form. Treat benches as OpenAI/third-party reports and keep scoring definitions when quoting.
