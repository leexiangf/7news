Anthropic is inviting life-sciences researchers to apply as guest investigators. Principal investigators and their teams at academic institutions, nonprofits, or government labs can propose projects to run with Anthropic researchers. Example areas include causal human biology, structural and computational molecular biology, medicinal chemistry, drug discovery, and clinical science. Proposals go through the official form.

The collaboration is heavier than handing out an API key. Anthropic describes an 8-to-12-week stretch in which its team runs models and harnesses at no cost. Both sides aim to produce analyses, artifacts, and tools, then publish them and open-source accompanying data and code. Rights are spelled out: guests keep rights to their own data throughout, and keep rights to independent follow-on research that grows out of the work.

Read it as a model company feeding real lab questions back into capabilities and workflows. Scientists still pose the problems; Anthropic supplies compute and an agent runtime. For AI4Science engineers, the near-term value is often not the slogan but whatever data shapes, tools, and reproducible pipelines may ship afterward. Deadlines, review criteria, and cohort size stay on the official call page; this article makes no extra promises.
