Google DeepMind introduced Gemini 4 Neon on 18 November 2026 as a frontier model that emphasizes end-to-end task completion speed across software engineering, knowledge work, and science workflows. The same post places it in the Gemini 4 family: Carbon for the heaviest long-horizon jobs, Hydrogen for everyday speed, and Neon for complex work that still needs to run often.

Pricing is explicit: **$3** per million input tokens (**$0.15** cached input, framed as about a 95% discount), **$15** per million output tokens, and a **1 million**-token context window. Four thinking levels let teams trade intelligence against spend. Google says that at the default high setting Neon pairs a strong Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score with roughly **32%** lower cost per task than Carbon, and that it sits near the Pareto frontier on Vals and Agent Arena. Those ranks and cost deltas are vendor-reported or third-party cites—align suites, thinking levels, and billing before comparing vendors.

The capability story clusters three ways. Enterprise work: synthesize spreadsheets, PDFs, charts, audio, and video inside one long context into structured deliverables. Coding: sustain tool-heavy agent loops, surgical multi-file edits, and Antigravity `/swarm` runs where Neon can orchestrate Hydrogen subagents. Security: relative results on benches such as AutoBaxBench, WebExploitBench, and SEC-bench Pro, with **Neon for cyber** said to reach trusted Fairwind defenders over the next few days. Safety sections claim Carbon-level severe-misuse and prompt-injection posture plus lower internal rates on cheating, overreach, and misaligned actions; treat the model card and safety report as the source of record for numbers.

Access is role-split: paid developers on the Gemini API (AI Studio, Android Studio); enterprises via Gemini for business; Pro and Ultra subscribers in Antigravity and the Gemini app; defenders via Fairwind. If Carbon already anchors your hardest jobs, Neon is the candidate default for high-frequency agentic coding and analysis—leave true ceiling work on Carbon, and watch cache discounts, thinking-level bills, and Fairwind seating.
