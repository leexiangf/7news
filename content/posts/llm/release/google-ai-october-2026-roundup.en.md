Google’s Keyword blog published its **October 2026 AI roundup**. For readers, it is a map of the month’s announcements; details still live in the linked posts.

**Models.** The recap says **Gemini 4 Argon** rolled out globally for developers, enterprises, and consumers across AI Studio, the Gemini API, Cloud, Search, the Gemini app, and robotics-oriented variants. About two weeks later Google announced **Gemini 4 Carbon**, framed as its strongest long-horizon engineering and cybersecurity model, now in **Fairwind** pre-release testing rather than general availability. **Genie 4** advances real-time interactive world simulation with physics, human motion, and native audio, steered by natural language.

**Safety and provenance.** **SynthID Detector** expands globally in English for checking whether image, video, or audio carries AI watermarks, including some partner content. Android adds an opt-in Enhanced protection mode in Messages (Private AI Compute plus cloud Gemini) aimed at conversational scams, alongside stronger call-forwarding and screen-sharing defenses. Health, education, and creative items (Fitbit Edge, AMIE clinical study, Playground, and more) appear with links in the original.

When citing numbers or availability, prefer the individual announcements over this digest’s framing.
