Vercel’s Changelog (**15 Nov 2026**) adds Alibaba’s **Qwen4-Max** to **AI Gateway** as `alibaba/qwen4-max`. It frames a reasoning model for coding and long-running agents with **image input**, a **1M**-token context, tool calling, and structured outputs—examples include driving Blender/Unity/Unreal/Godot with visual feedback fixes, and building cited reports across documents and images.

Alibaba reports about **35%** fewer tokens than **Qwen3.8-Max** on its **Work360** benchmark at high reasoning effort. Call it from the AI SDK, fx, or `npx vercel ai-gateway setup` for coding agents. This is a **Gateway availability** note; full model details sit with Alibaba.
