Google DeepMind introduced **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** on **12 Nov 2026** as the everyday “daily driver” for coding and **agentic** workflows. Where **Gemini 4 Carbon** and **Argon** lean into deep, long-horizon reasoning, Hydrogen emphasizes speed and efficiency—and can pair with frontier models in the same **Google Antigravity Orchestrator** session (near-Carbon success on Terminal-Bench 4.0 with ~**24%** less quota and ~**19%** faster completion, per Google).

Versus **Gemini 3.8 Flash**, Google cites ~**2.5×** faster task completion at the default high setting, Terminal-Bench 4.1 at about **57% vs 18.2%**, and more than **5×** fewer fabricated claims. On Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index it scores **51** at default high (above Argon’s 50) and **54** at **xhigh**. API pricing is flat across the full **1M**-token context: **$1.50 / $7.50** per million in/out. Thinking levels: low, medium, high (default), and xhigh. Availability spans AI Studio, the Gemini API, Gemini for business, the Gemini app, Search, and paid Antigravity tiers. Benchmarks and partner quotes are vendor- or third-party-reported.
