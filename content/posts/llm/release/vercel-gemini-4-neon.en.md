Vercel’s Changelog (**18 Nov 2026**) adds Google’s **Gemini 4 Neon** to **AI Gateway** as `google/gemini-4-neon`. It is framed as a major step up from the earlier lighter Gemini 4 tier—output quality closer to larger Gemini 4 models with faster responses—for coding, agents, and multi-step work.

It accepts text, images, video, and documents with a **1M**-token context, plus tool calling, structured outputs, Google Search and Maps grounding, and reasoning levels low / medium / high / xhigh. This is a **Gateway availability** note; the full model card sits with DeepMind’s launch post.
