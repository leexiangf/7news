OpenAI’s company post describes collaborations with Australian universities—including **UNSW Sydney**, the **University of Sydney**, **La Trobe**, and **Adelaide University**—to give students and staff access to frontier AI tools, with examples in clinical simulation, teacher training, and industry-linked projects. Campuses are also building frameworks for AI use in learning and assessment.

A commissioned survey of more than **1,000** Australian students (per OpenAI) finds 81% use AI several times a week (33% multiple times a day), 28% have already built an AI agent or assistant, and about 79% want more chances to apply advanced AI to real-world projects. Treat this as a **partnership and research narrative**, not a model release; cite the post’s footnotes for survey methods.
