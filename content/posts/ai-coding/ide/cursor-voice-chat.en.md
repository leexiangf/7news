Cursor’s changelog adds **realtime voice calls** with agents: describe a change out loud, ask what the agent is doing, and hear progress. Surfaces include the **Agents Window, IDE, web, and iOS**.

When the agent is idle and the chat input is empty, use the waveform **Start voice chat** control. On iOS you can also say “Hey Siri, call Cursor,” and mute or hang up from a Lock Screen Live Activity.

Treat it as an interaction-channel update; permissions, regions, and quotas follow Cursor’s plan docs.
