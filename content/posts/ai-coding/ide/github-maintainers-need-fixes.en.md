GitHub’s open-source channel argues that as AI lowers the cost of filing issues, maintainers can drown in low-quality or duplicate reports. What stays scarce is **mergeable fixes** and responsible collaboration—not more auto-generated tickets.

Tool builders should bias scanners toward patches, minimal repros, and verified fixes rather than issue volume. Collaboration guidance and examples are in GitHub’s original post.
