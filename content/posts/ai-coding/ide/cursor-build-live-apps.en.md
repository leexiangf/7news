Cursor’s changelog notes that agents lowered the cost of writing code, yet many tools never leave localhost. The new path lets you **build live apps** in Cursor and share them with a link—describe the goal, and the product handles build, database, auth, and deploy.

Start a **cloud agent** and say explicitly that you want an app. The audience includes non-specialist builders inside an org shipping small internal tools. Update the Cursor client; quotas, billing, and residency follow Cursor’s docs.
