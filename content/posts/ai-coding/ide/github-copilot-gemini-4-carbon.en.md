GitHub’s Changelog makes **Gemini 4 Carbon** generally available in **GitHub Copilot**, aimed at complex coding, agentic, and reasoning work with a security-focused framing. Usage is billed at provider list pricing under usage-based billing.

It is available to **Pro+, Max, Business, and Enterprise** users in the model picker across VS Code, Visual Studio, Copilot CLI, coding agent, the Copilot app, github.com, mobile, JetBrains, Xcode, and Eclipse. Rollout is gradual. Business and Enterprise admins can govern access through Copilot model policy.
