GitHub’s Changelog (**18 Nov 2026**) makes **team customizations** **GA** for organization teams. Team maintainers and org owners open the team’s **Customize Copilot** tab and pick approved **Agent Plugins** as team defaults from built-in, enterprise, or team-added marketplaces.

Selected plugins are enabled by default for the team’s **direct** members in VS Code, the GitHub Copilot app, and Copilot CLI—child-team members do not inherit them. Members can turn a team plugin off where policy allows. Enterprise and organization policies still win on allowed marketplaces and required/blocked plugins. Available on **Copilot Business** and **Enterprise**. Team-config note.
