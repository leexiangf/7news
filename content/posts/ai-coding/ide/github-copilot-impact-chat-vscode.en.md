GitHub’s Changelog updates the **Copilot impact dashboard**: the Agent adoption chart now includes **Copilot Chat** and the **VS Code Agents** window—nine experiences total. The “Passive users” cohort is renamed **Chat-first users**: active Copilot users who haven’t used a qualifying feature on at least two days in 28 days, so they don’t enter an adoption phase. Enterprise and organization 28-day reports add `chat` and `vscode_agent` under `totals_by_feature`.

Chat and VS Code Agents **do not** count toward adoption phases. Engagement still means use on at least two days in the window. Older reports keep the original seven features. Access follows the usual Copilot Metrics permissions and policy.
