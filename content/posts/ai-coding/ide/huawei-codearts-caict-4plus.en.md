Huawei Cloud says its **CodeArts** coding agent passed CAICT’s Trusted AI **enterprise development agent** assessment at the current top **4+** grade, among the first domestic vendors through that program (**13 Nov 2026**). The rubric covers requirements analysis, codegen/completion, unit tests, vulnerability fixes, and R&D engineeringization end to end.

Product framing: open SOTA plus Huawei models, including a HarmonyOS **ArkTS** coding model; modes include **Vibe Coding**, spec-driven **SDD**, an **Agent Team** of about **16** specialist agents, and ten-million-scale **Codebase** indexing; engineering hooks into GitHub/Jenkins and enterprise pipelines, with quality agents and sandbox/high-risk command controls. HarmonyOS claims cite **100k+** training samples, **>80%** fewer errors per thousand lines, and **>78%** first-compile pass rates (vendor figures). An earlier CAICT 4+ covered intelligent testing; this extends certification across the toolchain. Treat as a **certification release**—verify details in CAICT/Huawei materials.
