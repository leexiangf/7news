At Ignite 2026, Microsoft Foundry expands **Browser Automation** for agentic workloads that still live in browsers—internal portals, vendor consoles, and legacy apps with no API.

The post highlights a larger set of **MCP tools** for browser work: open and read pages, accessibility snapshots, click/type/forms, dialogs, tabs, uploads, plus screenshots and console/network capture. A sample agent processes invoices, validates rules, flags anomalies in Excel, and enters results into an expense portal. When state is ambiguous, **Live View** shows the headed browser in real time; **Take control** lets a person complete sign-in, approvals, or recovery.

Treat it as a way to put “must click the UI” steps into the agent toolbox with human co-driving. Permissions, residency, and pricing follow Foundry docs.
