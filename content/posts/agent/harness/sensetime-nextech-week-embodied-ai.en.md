SenseTime says it joined **NexTech Week TOKYO 2026 Autumn** with ecosystem partners to show embodied AI, industrial inspection, and automated data capture for retail, manufacturing, and campus use, while exploring Japan-local collaboration. APAC president Shi Jun’s quote frames a shift from “technology enabler” to “ecosystem enabler,” combining vision AI, generative AI, and Physical AI.

On the booth: **SenseMart Go** robotic retail stores (order, pick, deliver; 24-hour ops in the release); **Xiaotu** open-scene autonomy; **Kaiwu world model 3.1**; **ACE Ego Kit** capture gear; and industrial **TX01** dual-arm robots with grasp and imitation-learning claims beside vision stacks. SenseTime notes a Japan office since 2016. Treat this as **show news**, not a single-SKU GA; commercial scope stays with the original Chinese release.
