Vercel’s Changelog (**13 Nov 2026**) adds Parallel’s **Decisions Fast** to **AI Gateway** as `parallel/parallel-decisions-fast`, **free through 23 Nov 2026**, with no separate Parallel account. It searches the **live web** to answer typed questions about companies, products, and policies, returning probabilities plus cited excerpts for CRM enrichment, eligibility checks, lead routing, or claim verification.

Question types include Boolean, Choice, and Score; related questions can share context and evidence in one request. Call it via AI SDK `experimental_decide`, a TypeSafe-compatible API, or the OpenAI-compatible Decisions API. Citations (URL, optional title, excerpts) apply to the **whole request**, not per answer. Traffic shows in Gateway logs and budgets. This is a **time-boxed availability** note.
