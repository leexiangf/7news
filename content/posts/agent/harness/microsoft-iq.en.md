Microsoft’s Azure Blog (Yitzhak Kesselman, **17 Nov 2026**) introduces **Microsoft IQ**, an enterprise **context management** platform that stitches **Work IQ, Fabric IQ, Foundry IQ, and Web IQ** with **350+** third-party connectors—arguing advantage lives in processes, institutional knowledge, and systems of record, not in the shared frontier model.

**Business plugins** package that context, skills, and authoritative sources for reuse in Microsoft Copilot, GitHub Copilot, Foundry (via MCP), and ChatGPT / Claude marketplaces. Same-day highlights include Work IQ Business Applications, Retrieve, and real-time meeting context at GA; Foundry IQ knowledge graphs and **Microsoft Retriever 1** in preview, with **Foundry IQ Serverless** slated for **Dec 2026** GA; and **Web IQ** open to every Azure customer with no waitlist, pricing from about **$1 per 1,000** calls, plus Places/Weather, Scopes, and BYOL. AEMO’s NEM-GEM is the customer spotlight. Treat this as an **Ignite platform** piece; GA/preview flags for each IQ live in the linked deep-dives.
