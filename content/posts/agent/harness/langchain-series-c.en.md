LangChain announced a **$300M** Series C at a **$3B** valuation. The thesis: every company will need to own and govern its agent systems—choose models freely, control data and infra, and improve agents continuously under security constraints.

The product centerpiece is **LangSmith**, described as a framework-agnostic platform to run, improve, and govern agents. LangChain cites more than **7,000** organizations and names customers such as Harvey, Rippling, NVIDIA, Toyota, Salesforce, Clay, and Workday. Pillars: Agent Runtime, Agent Improvement, Agent Governance. Investors and use of proceeds stay in the original post.

For platform readers, this is funding plus positioning—not a proof of product quality by valuation alone.
