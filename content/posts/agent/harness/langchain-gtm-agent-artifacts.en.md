LangChain’s blog explains how its internal **GTM Agent** produces consistent, on-brand sales artifacts. Over **12 weeks** it says reps saved substantial hand-assembly time across **1,540** artifacts (account plans, proof-of-value docs, proposal decks, onboarding plans), running about **100+/week** lately, with customer-sent pieces accounting for roughly **337** hours of rep time saved.

Key results (company-reported, matched deals): deals where a rep sent an artifact moved to a later stage about **47%** of the time versus about **16%** without; about **46%** of artifacts were followed by a customer call within **14 days**. Quality is framed as **business context** more than prompt tuning—Salesforce, usage, call transcripts, stage gates, and “brand as code” templates—plus proactive suggestions for missing docs while a deal is still live. Readers can reuse the pattern on **Deep Agents** / **Managed Deep Agents** (sandbox, skills, memory, scheduled runs). Treat the lift numbers as internal ops stats, not a public A/B study.
