ByteDance’s Seed team launched **Seedream 5.5** (blog dated **9 Nov 2026**). The release highlights three upgrades: **pixel-level preservation** of unedited regions across multi-round edits—claiming up to about **100** continuous rounds on one artwork with less color drift and detail loss; **native RGBA** transparent/semi-transparent assets without post-hoc matting; and more natural lighting, materials, and skin texture plus sharper complex glyphs and dense small text. Seed says overall quality beats the prior generation and leads peers on text structure and photoreal text-to-image (no public scorecard attached).

Rollout covers Jimeng, Doubao app/web, Doubao Work skills, and Volcano Ark’s Doubao-Seedream-5.5. Treat as a **product launch**; quotas and regions follow each surface.
