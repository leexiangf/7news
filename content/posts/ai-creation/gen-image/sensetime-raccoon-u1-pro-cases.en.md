SenseTime published the second “Awaken the Raccoon” feature, focusing on **Raccoon** image generation backed by the **SenseNova U-series / U1 Pro** native multimodal model. The pitch is handling layered instructions with multi-turn revision rather than one-shot prompts.

Two user stories illustrate the claim. A hobbyist builds a three-step workflow—explore directions, lock design details, then prune conflicting cues—and reports stronger Chinese-style control on clothing structure and poetic scenes without drifting into overly 3D looks. A university media instructor turns WCS/ERP/OMS process notes into scene graphs so stakeholders can grasp the workflow visually.

Treat the piece as **official product storytelling**, not an independent benchmark. Use it as color on how U1 Pro is being positioned for iterative image work; verify capability claims against model cards and your own tests.
