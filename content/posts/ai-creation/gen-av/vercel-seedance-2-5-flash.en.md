Vercel’s Changelog (**16 Nov 2026**) adds ByteDance’s **Seedance 2.5 Flash** to **AI Gateway** as `bytedance/seedance-2.5-flash`. It is the faster, lower-cost sibling of Seedance 2.5; Vercel says output stays close on human realism, reference-based editing, and prompt adherence.

Generate **audio-synced** clips from text or from image, video, and audio references—**4–30** seconds, up to **720p / 24 fps**, landscape, square, or portrait. You can also edit or extend existing videos, or supply images for first and last frames. Samples use **AI SDK 7** `experimental_generateVideo` with `inputReferences` for media. This is a **Gateway availability** note; commercial terms and full capability cards sit with ByteDance and Gateway docs.
