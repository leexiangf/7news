Google DeepMind updated Project Genie and made **Genie 4** available. It remains an experimental path for generating and exploring interactive digital worlds, answering early feedback that users wanted deeper in-world control and easier building and sharing.

The post highlights natural-language live edits (weather, characters), adaptive audio, and a creative agent that builds complex environments through conversation. A community gallery lets Ultra subscribers build, share, and remix worlds. Access: **eligible Google AI Ultra** subscribers begin rolling out; **Google AI Pro** can join the waitlist on the Project Genie site.

For creators, treat this as a world-generation channel—not a text-LLM release. Quotas, regions, and feature limits follow the Project Genie site and subscription terms.
