SenseTime’s release (**16 Nov 2026**) congratulates the AI short *Invincible Superman* (director “小黄导 AIGC”) for winning the **annual technology application award** in the 39th China Golden Rooster Awards’ 2026 Future Imaging AI Unit, and says its **Seko** AI video platform supplied creation tools and compute.

The pitch is that Seko stitches storyboards, character/scene assets, and video generation—an “one-person crew” framing. Shot-level share, model versions, and capacity figures are not disclosed. Treat as an **awards partnership note**; technical credit belongs with the filmmakers and award records.
