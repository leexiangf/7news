SenseTime’s Chinese news page (**17 Nov 2026**) covers a VR large-space immersion of Nanjing Museum’s treasure map **Kunyu Wanguo Quantu**, shown in the digital zone of the **11th China Museum and Related Products & Technologies Expo** in Haikou. The museum leads research, creative direction, and operations; SenseTime supplies digital content and **AI+VR** implementation. Visitors wear headsets—no phone UI—and walk a 100+ m² narrative built from the digitized map.

The physical Ming-dynasty colored scroll is a short-display treasure; the digital build aims at lasting access and “walking into” the 1602 Ricci–Li Zhizao world view. The release cites **280,000+** visits since the full experience opened in **July 2025**, framing it as a first-of-kind 8K cinematic digital-heritage VR show. Tech callouts include large-space tracking, 720° panoramas, AIGC characters/scenes, and 8K presentation, plus claimed **~10×** art-production speed and modeling from **1–2 days** to **1–2 hours**. Full and slim modules are pitched for touring. Treat attendance and efficiency figures as **project/vendor claims**.
