NVIDIA’s blog (**16 Nov 2026**) centers operations chief **Deb Shoquist**, who runs the supply chain and manufacturing behind AI platforms. Not a new SKU sheet—an explanation of what partner relationships make year-round tech introductions possible.

## Three legs of a partner match

Shoquist’s bar: **technical skill, financial/capacity strength to fund ramps, and willingness to do what has never been built**. Missing one leg may mean investing to create a partner—optical names Coherent and Lumentum are cited as tech-and-willingness-first, scale still building; CPO pulls scale partners new to optics into the chain.

Long relationships named: TSMC since **1998**; SPIL and KYEC assembling/testing for more than two decades; plus Foxconn, Wistron, Unimicron, Amkor, and peers. She spends about six weeks a year on factory floors in Taiwan, China, Korea, Vietnam, and the U.S., with quarterly key-supplier meetings.

## Checkable floor details

| Scene | Claim |
| --- | --- |
| Amphenol cable cartridge | ~**1,500** small legs; quality target under **1 DPPM**; 100+ automated lines |
| Chip allocation | Weekly assignment by factory yield and time-of-ownership; die ID through customer invoice |
| Cycle time | TSMC chip-out → Foxconn/Wistron system-out from ~**five weeks to ~two** when needed |
| GB200 ramp | Auto handler under plan → revert to manual load with hundreds of operators; NVIDIA “swarm” + Foxconn automation redesign before full scale |
