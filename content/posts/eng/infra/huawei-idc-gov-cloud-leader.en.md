Huawei Cloud’s news post (**17 Nov 2026**) says IDC’s *MarketScape: China Government Smart Cloud Solutions Vendor Assessment 2026* places Huawei Cloud in the **Leaders** category and #1 on both **market share** and **product capability**. IDC’s market framing (cloud services evolving into AI capability platforms; focus on data governance and agents) is also relayed—cite as Huawei Cloud’s summary of IDC.

## Agentic Cloud three-link story

Huawei Cloud Stack narrative spans compute, data, and agents:

| Link | Vendor claims |
| --- | --- |
| Government smart compute | NPU pooling + memory snapshots; utilization ~**30%→70%**; A5 supernode support |
| Trusted AI data space | 120+ operators, 25+ mainstream models; retrieval precision ~**50%→85%**; privacy compute / data capsules / blockchain |
| Agent platform | openJiuwen; Skill Turbo ~**−30%** latency, ~**−50%** tokens |

Longgang “Longyuan” platform: 14 domains, 21 legacy systems, 53 Skills, 94 tools, 30+ apps—case figures. Closing stats: Stack serving **1500+** government clouds, **160+** countries, **5500+** customers.

Analyst quadrants are not lab benches; dual “#1” belongs to the IDC original. Utilization and retrieval figures are vendor messaging—do not treat them as independently verified absolutes.
