NVIDIA’s corporate blog (**10 Nov 2026**) uses COP31’s zero-waste framing to spotlight four **Inception** members applying vision AI and robotics to mixed waste streams. Sustainability head Josh Parker says AI helps identify what can be recovered and automate sorting. Case roundup—not a product release.

## Four snapshots (company claims)

| Company | Hardware / stack | Headline claims |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AIRECO** (Taiwan) | Jetson Orin NX + robot arms | Tainan pilot recognition >**95%**; plan **18k→48k tons/year**, ~**−85%** opex |
| **EverestLabs** (US) | Edge RTX + data-center GPU training | ~**150** robots; ~**40B** objects, ~**25k** tons recycled, ~**30k** t CO₂e avoided |
| **Greyparrot** (UK) | GPU training + Jetson edge | **300+** systems, **20+** countries; 15-ton audit **375h→6h**; ~**10–30%** efficiency; Amcor food-grade yield ~**+12%** |
| **reverse.fashion** (Berlin) | TensorRT + Jetson, dozen+ models | Belt ~**3 m/s**; one hour ≈ prior week of manual sort |

Greyparrot also cites belts up to ~**4 m/s** and a trillion+ bounding boxes. Background: under **7%** of materials entering the economy are recycled/reused; municipal solid waste **2B+** tons/year—separate industry context from company self-tests.
