Huawei Cloud’s news post (**9 Nov 2026**) says IDC’s *MarketScape: China Industrial AI and Agent Platform Vendor Assessment 2026* places it in the **Leaders** quadrant and #1 on both **market share** and **product capability**. IDC’s stage framing—from point pilots to platform-scale deploy—is relayed, not independently verified here.

## Three platform layers + industrial cases

| Layer | Vendor notes |
| --- | --- |
| Agentic Infra | A5 supernode; NPU pooling + memory snapshots; utilization ~**30%→70%** |
| Data chain | Compute–storage split, AI DataLake, DataArts—“manage / supply / use / circulate” |
| AI platform | Train–infer integration + openJiuwen hybrid-cloud agent enablement |

| Customer | Claim |
| --- | --- |
| NISCO “Yuan Ye” | 25 business agents; blast-furnace temperature prediction >**90%** |
| Conch Cement | 5 categories, 40+ scenes; clinker strength prediction >**85%**; ~**−1%** standard coal use at top energy-efficiency tier |

Closing scale stats recycle the Stack narrative (1500+ government clouds, 500+ financial clouds, 65 mega SOEs, etc.). The piece argues dual #1 comes from an evolvable, governable, scalable hybrid-cloud platform—not a single model win. Still cross-check the IDC original for quadrant and dimension definitions.
