SenseTime’s newsroom post says **Raccoon** placed on both consumer and enterprise third-party charts. On the consumer side it cites “AI Xinbang – AI Product Chart” week 104 (Oct 26–Nov 1): global #16 / China #8 after a 33-place weekly jump, with weekly visits over **20.45 million**—third among domestic products, per the release.

On the enterprise side it cites two 2026 Frost & Sullivan studies: China enterprise desktop agents, Raccoon third alongside WorkBuddy and Qwen Office; and China enterprise analytics agents, where it claims the only Leaders spot for “standalone application-style analytics agents,” ranked first. All figures are the vendor’s restatement of third-party rankings; methods and comparability follow the original reports.
