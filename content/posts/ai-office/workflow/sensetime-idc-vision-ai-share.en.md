SenseTime’s release cites IDC’s *China AI Software Market Half-Year Tracker, 26H1*: in H1 2026 China’s **computer-vision private** market grew **16.8%** YoY to **RMB 43.2 billion**, with SenseTime at **19.5%** share in first place. The company also claims a decade (2016–2025) atop related IDC China vision-AI rankings.

The strategic frame is **CV 2.0 / Agentic CV**—specialist vision models, the Monolith algorithm factory, SenseNova multimodal models, and agent infrastructure—packaged as city/enterprise AI platforms, Nebula edge products, and Raccoon for government data. A flood-response vignette argues for active alerting and natural-language task orchestration over wall-watching. Mid-year results cited: vision AI in **20+** countries, **4,500+** customers, ~**67%** revenue from existing clients. Treat as a **share-and-strategy release**; verify IDC tables in the original report.
