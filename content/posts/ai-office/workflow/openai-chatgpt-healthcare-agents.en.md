OpenAI’s post (**16 Nov 2026**) updates **ChatGPT for Healthcare** with stronger **computer-use** agents that operate existing healthcare software—reading screens, clicking, typing, and moving between apps—plus improved trusted clinical search with citations, and **20+** new healthcare plugins (UpToDate, Luma, Metriport, Stedi, TriZetto, Amion, Aiva, and peers), including ChartGPT-in-EHR flows.

Customer callouts include Abridge, Ambience, Cigna Healthcare, and CVS Health on referrals and document handoffs. In a blind eval, physicians gave **1,300+** reviews rating the updated experience higher across all eight dimensions, including factual correctness, safety, actionability, and source authoritativeness (vendor claim). Content is not used for training; PHI workflows need BAA-covered eligible services plus security and retention controls. Vertical product update—not a consumer ChatGPT default.
