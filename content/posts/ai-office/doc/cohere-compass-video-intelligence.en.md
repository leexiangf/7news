Cohere (**16 Nov 2026**) previews **Video Intelligence** in **Compass**: agents and search apps can find **precise moments** in meetings, training libraries, and media archives—understanding speech and on-screen content, returning timestamped clips with transcript and visual description, in the same permission-aware index as documents.

Moments are bounded at query time rather than relying on fixed segments; results plug into RAG and agent workflows, and the Compass MCP server can expose slides and charts. Cohere reports average nDCG@10 about **4.4** points ahead of the strongest alternative across 11 datasets (vendor eval). Design partners can run managed cloud or in-house. Product preview and recruitment—not general availability.
