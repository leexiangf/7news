## How it usually breaks

AI drafts a plan that looks complete. Three people each edit one section. Nobody can say which sentences are facts and which are guesses.

## Minimum process

1. AI writes only in a draft zone
2. The conclusion zone needs an owner-style confirmation
3. External numbers must carry source links
4. Before publish, run a counter-example question pass

Tools can change. Rules have to land first.
